Side B — Our Story

Started in a garage. Still run like one.

Groove & Co. began as a folding table at a Sunday flea market — a hundred records, a borrowed extension cord, and a portable player nobody could turn off. Fifteen years later we still buy the way we started: crate by crate, from estate sales, closing shops, and collectors who trust us to give their records a second life.

Every sleeve is inspected by hand. Every turntable that leaves our bench has been played before it's packed. We're not chasing scale — we're chasing the right copy of the right pressing for the right listener.
